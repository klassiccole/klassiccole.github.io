Espresso

The foundation of everything at The Fired Bean. The dragon insists on a fine, even grind and a level puck — tamping crooked is a fireable offense, which around here is literal. (The founder's home setup, for the record, is a stovetop moka pot and a Café Bustelo brick. The dragon pretends not to judge.)

dose:        18 g, fine grind
yield:       36 g
time:        25-30 seconds
temperature: 200°F / 93°C
pressure:    9 bars

Cappuccino

Equal parts, no debate. A proper cappuccino is one-third espresso, one-third steamed milk, one-third foam. If you can pour it without a spoon holding the foam back, the dragon nods approvingly.

espresso:    1 shot (36 g)
steamed milk: 1 part
foam:        1 part, dense
cup:         6 oz ceramic

Latte

For customers who want the espresso to whisper instead of roar. Steam the milk to satin — glossy, not bubbly — and pour from low to build the rosetta.

espresso:    1-2 shots
steamed milk: 6-8 oz
foam:        0.5 cm, microfoam
pours:       low start, finish high

Pour Over

The patient method. Blooming the grounds wakes the bean up; the spiral pour keeps the bed flat. Best with the Sunrise Ethiopian — its fruit notes open up fully.

dose:        22 g, medium-fine
water:       350 g at 205°F
bloom:       50 g for 45 seconds
total time:  3:00-3:30

Cold Brew

Coarse grind, cold water, and a full day of patience — the one menu item the dragon cannot rush by breathing on it. Serve over ice, cut with water to taste.

ratio:       1:8 coffee to water
grind:       coarse
steep:       18-24 hours, refrigerated
serve:       over ice, dilute 1:1

Steam Wand Care

Milk left on a wand is a betrayal of every drink that follows. Purge, wipe, and purge again after every single pitcher — the checklist is posted over the machine and enforced by fire.

1. purge:    1 second, before steaming
2. steam:    tip just below the surface
3. purge:    full blast after steaming
4. wipe:     damp cloth, while wand is hot
