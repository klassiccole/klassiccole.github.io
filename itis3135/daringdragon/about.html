About

Who We Are

Daring Dragon is a one-person print shop in Charlotte, North Carolina, run by a photographer and tinkerer who kept bringing home odd filament colors and needed something to print with them. Dragons were the obvious answer. Then came heroes. Then came a booth at a night market, and it stopped being a hobby.

Everything is printed on a single workhorse printer, which keeps the catalog honest: if it cannot pass a hand inspection under good light, it does not ship. The same eye that shoots the product photos is the one that checks every layer line.

Why Miniatures

A good miniature makes a table feel real. The moment someone sets a dragon on the map, the story stops being abstract and everyone leans in. We print so that more tables get that moment, without anyone paying boutique prices for a figurine that will take a sword to the face by session three.

That is also why the sculpts are sturdy and the prices are plain: these are game pieces first and display pieces second. Paint them, drop them, let the bard knock them over. They are built for it.
